### Full Description
```text
Aradhana Studio Remote (by HamroCMS) turns your Android phone or tablet into a fast, wireless controller and stage prompter for Aradhana Studio Desktop Presentation Software.

Designed specifically for Nepali and global churches, worship leaders, pastors, and media operators — it works 100% offline over your church Wi-Fi router or Mobile Hotspot with zero internet required!

KEY FEATURES:
• Instant Secure QR Pairing: Scan the QR code on Aradhana Studio Desktop (v1.7.32+) to pair securely over local Wi-Fi or offline Mobile Hotspot.
• Live Slide & Projector Control: Advance slides, jump to Chorus/Verses, use Blind Swipe Pad mode, or navigate hands-free using your phone's Volume Up/Down keys.
• Built-in Nepali Phonetic Typing: Type Romanized Nepali (e.g., "yeshule", "stutiko") with an instant horizontal Nepali suggestion bar — no external keyboard setup needed.
• Complete Nepali Bhajan & Chorus Control: Search 1,800+ Nepali Bhajans, Choruses, and Bal Sangati songs by number, Devanagari, or Roman text, or dial any hymn number directly to go live.
• Fast Nepali & English Bible Projection: Select Bible translation, Book, Chapter, and single or multiple Verse numbers, or search scripture instantly to project onto the church screen.
• Full-Screen Podium Stage Prompter: Turn any phone or tablet on the pulpit into a live confidence monitor with large lyrics, next-slide preview, touch-lock protection, and preaching countdown timer.
• Stage Alerts & Laser Pointer: Send silent stage messages to the preacher, start preaching timers, or highlight scripture on the projector using the 16:9 laser/spotlight touchpad.

PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST:
• Connects exclusively to private local network / hotspot IPs using 192-bit cryptographic session tokens.
• Zero ads, zero analytics trackers, and zero personal data uploaded to external servers.

Download Aradhana Studio Desktop exclusively from our official website: https://hamrocms.com/download
```
